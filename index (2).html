Featured · CapstoneP-01

AutoRC — Autonomous RC Vehicle

A Raspberry Pi 4B-based autonomous RC car that uses a forward-facing OV5647 camera and a behaviour-cloned CNN to predict steering commands from track images.

  • Steering servo driven through a PCA9685 PWM controller; drive motor through a BTS7960 driver — no Arduino in the final driving loop.
  • Kotlin / Jetpack Compose Android app for live camera view, manual control and autonomous-mode initialisation, backed by Flask control and video-streaming endpoints.
  • Trained over 30 epochs; best validation MSE reached at epoch 11.
1,620Labelled pairs
0.174Best val MSE
16.66°Steering MAE
25.03°Steering RMSE

Ultrasonic obstacle detection is an experimental add-on; the integrated controller is still being refined through track testing.
